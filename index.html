Memdeklaro

Decentralized Identity
Done Right

Memdeklaro is a public domain initiative building a framework for decentralized and self-asserted identity.

Generate a self-declaration of identity using the client-side web app - free to use, privacy-friendly and zero third parties.

Express your chosen name and personal ties, independent from centralized third parties. Make connections, build up your reputation and participate in communities online and in person, using a physical self-declaration card or digital credential.
